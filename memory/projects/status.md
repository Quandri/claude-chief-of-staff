## Guidelines

- One section per active project
- Update status on each `/sync` from Slack, email, and meeting sources
- Move to Recently Completed when done (keep for 30 days, then remove)
- Flag blockers prominently — these drive priority
- Cross-reference with `goals.yaml` for goal alignment
- Cross-reference with `decisions/log.md` for project-related decisions
- Keep updates factual — "80% complete per eng update" not "seems almost done"
