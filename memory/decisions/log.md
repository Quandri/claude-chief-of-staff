## Guidelines

- Only log decisions that affect strategy, resources, or commitments
- Include the "why" — rationale is more valuable than the decision itself
- Update outcomes when known (Pending → Successful / Failed / Revised)
- Keep to one line per decision — link to emails/docs for detail
- Source decisions from: email threads, meeting notes, Slack conversations
- Remove decisions older than 6 months unless outcome is still pending or decision is still referenced
