## Guidelines

- Record observed behavior, not just stated preferences (actions > words)
- Date entries — preferences evolve
- Note when a preference is context-dependent ("prefers Slack for quick questions, email for decisions")
- These supplement the hard constraints in CLAUDE.md Part 2
- After a preference is confirmed 3+ times, suggest adding it to CLAUDE.md
- Remove preferences that become inconsistent
