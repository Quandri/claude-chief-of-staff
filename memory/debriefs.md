## Guidelines

- Keep each daily entry to 10-15 lines max
- Entries older than 14 days should be archived (delete from this file — they've already been consumed by /weekly)
- Focus on what's useful for weekly synthesis: where time went, what moved, what didn't
- The "carry forward" section is the bridge between days — /gm can reference it
- "What didn't" is as important as "what moved" — it surfaces drift from goals
