## Guidelines

- Only record information confirmed across multiple sources (meetings, emails, documents)
- Date every entry — strategy context decays fast
- Focus on "what changed" rather than restating known facts
- Remove entries older than 90 days unless still actively relevant
- Never speculate — only record what was explicitly stated or decided
